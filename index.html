What I Offer

Practical, evidence-based support for dyslexia and literacy difficulties, tailored to each learner.

Dyslexia Assessment

Full diagnostic assessment for children and adults, with a clear written report and recommendations.

Structured Literacy

One-to-one, multisensory teaching to build reading, spelling and writing skills.

Handwriting Groups

Small groups focused on letter formation, fluency and confidence on the page.

Social Communication Groups

LEGO-based groups that build social communication skills in a relaxed, structured setting.

About Jennie

Jennie McAleese is a qualified specialist practitioner holding an approved qualification in SpLD Dyslexia assessment & intervention. Jennie holds a current Specific Learning Difficulties Assessment Practising Certificate with the professional association of teachers of students with specific learning difficulties in the UK, a regulatory body of standards in practice.

  • Member of the British Dyslexia Association
  • Current SpLD Assessment Practising Certificate holder
  • Postgraduate Certificate in Specific Learning Difficulties, Dyslexia — awarded by Edge Hill University
