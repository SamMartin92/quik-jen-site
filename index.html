Our Services

Dyslexia Assessment

Online & In-person (UK & Ireland)

A comprehensive diagnostic process including:

  • Pre-assessment information gathering
  • Diagnostic assessment
  • Detailed written report
  • Feedback consultation session

Specialist Tuition

Individual (Online & In-person)

Available in blocks of intervention. Sessions agreed in advance. Includes structured literacy & study skills.

Group (In-person)

  • Screening assessment in literacy
