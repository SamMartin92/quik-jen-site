Consultations

Parent Consultation (30 minutes)

At times, it can be helpful to discuss literacy concerns in a confidential space. Parent consultation is available face-to-face, online or by telephone.

Literacy Consultation

Available online & in-person. A literacy consultation may involve a combination of:

  • Information gathering
  • Screening assessment in literacy
  • Specific subtests to assess current abilities

A concise written report detailing results and recommendations (as appropriate) will be delivered within 10 working days.
