Dyslexia Assessments

Clear answers, a full written report and practical next steps.

What is a Dyslexia Assessment?

A dyslexia assessment is a full diagnostic assessment carried out by a specialist practitioner. It looks at reading, writing, spelling and the underlying cognitive processing skills that support literacy, using standardised, age-appropriate assessment tools.

The assessment identifies specific strengths and difficulties, and whether these are consistent with a diagnosis of dyslexia or another specific learning difficulty.

  • Carried out by a qualified SpLD specialist practitioner
  • Suitable for children and adults
  • Uses recognised, standardised assessment tools
  • Results explained in plain, jargon-free language

Who It's For

An assessment can help at any age or stage.

Children

For a child who is struggling with reading, spelling or writing despite good teaching and support at school.

Students

For students who need an up-to-date report to apply for exam or exam-access arrangements in school or college.

Adults

For adults who suspect they may be dyslexic, or who need a current assessment for work or further study.
